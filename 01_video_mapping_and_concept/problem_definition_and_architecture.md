# Problem Definition & Taxonomy Architecture

## 1. Problem Definition
Higher education institutions struggle with late identification of student burnout, cognitive overload, and academic distress. MindTrack AI provides an automated NLP pipeline that detects early warning signs from natural language reflections and survey text.

## 2. Multi-Class Label Taxonomy

1. **High Stress (Index: 80 - 100)**: Acute emotional distress, anxiety, sleep deprivation, panic symptoms regarding coursework.
2. **Overload (Index: 65 - 85)**: Volume-based distress, overlapping assignment due dates, excessive reading.
3. **Frustration (Index: 50 - 70)**: Friction with grading transparency, LMS platform failures, slow teaching assistant communication.
4. **Disengagement (Index: 40 - 60)**: Apathy, loss of academic motivation, skipped lectures.
5. **Neutral (Index: 20 - 35)**: Purely factual statements, syllabus dates, logistical observations.
6. **Positive (Index: 0 - 20)**: High engagement, appreciation for teaching quality, balanced coursework.
