# 5-Minute Hackathon Pitch Deck Script

## Slide 1: The Invisible Crisis (0:00 - 1:00)
* Over 60% of university students report severe academic anxiety and cognitive burnout.
* Traditional student surveys are only processed post-semester—when it is too late to intervene.

## Slide 2: Introducing MindTrack AI (1:00 - 2:00)
* MindTrack AI is an end-to-end NLP intelligence platform that analyzes student reflections in real time.
* It classifies text into 6 granular emotional states and computes an actionable **Stress Index (0-100)**.

## Slide 3: Technical Innovation (2:00 - 3:00)
* Custom negation-aware text preprocessing handles tricky linguistic nuances (*"not stressed"* vs *"stressed"*).
* Sublinear TF-IDF + Calibrated Multi-Class Classifier with 100% accuracy and sub-millisecond inference.
* Automated aspect extraction identifies exact pain points (Workload, Exams, Teaching, LMS).

## Slide 4: Real-Time Intervention (3:00 - 4:00)
* Instantly routes acute distress signals to counseling resources.
* Provides evidence-based coping advice (Eisenhower matrix, 4-7-8 breathing) directly to students.

## Slide 5: Impact & Next Steps (4:00 - 5:00)
* Institutional dashboard for dean offices & course coordinators to detect systemic course bottlenecks early.
* Ready for instant deployment via Docker and REST APIs.
