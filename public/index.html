    🧘‍♀️

    4-7-8 Breathing Technique

    Inhale quietly through your nose for 4 seconds, hold your breath for 7 seconds, and exhale completely through your mouth for 8 seconds. Reduces pre-exam autonomic arousal.

    ⏱️

    Pomodoro Work Pacing

    Study in 25-minute undistracted focus blocks followed by 5-minute cognitive breaks. After 4 cycles, take a longer 20-minute rest to prevent mental fatigue.

    📊

    Eisenhower Priority Matrix

    Separate deliverables into: 1) Urgent & Important (immediate focus), 2) Important but Not Urgent (scheduled revision), 3) Urgent Not Important (delegate/streamline).
